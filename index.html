About Me

My research and applied work sits at the intersection of machine learning evaluation and AI model reasoning — I care as much about whether a result is trustworthy as I do about the headline accuracy number. That means scrutinising near-perfect scores for leakage and bias, using leave-family-out and stratified testing to check real generalisation, and applying explainability tools such as Grad-CAM to audit what a model has actually learned.

Before moving into full-time data science and ML engineering, I spent two years as a visiting lecturer teaching Web Development, Android Mobile Application Development and Operating Systems, and several years as a software engineer building real-time, cross-platform applications. I'm currently based in Glasgow, United Kingdom, and available remotely on a flexible schedule.
